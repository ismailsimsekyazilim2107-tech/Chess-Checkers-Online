Chess & Checkers Online

Privacy Policy explaining how information is handled when you use Chess & Checkers Online, including online gameplay, accounts, chat, voice communication, advertising and purchases.

Last Updated: October 4, 2026
Important: Chess & Checkers Online uses third-party services to provide authentication, online multiplayer functionality, cloud data storage, advertising, purchases and real-time voice communication. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information involved and how those services are used.

1 Introduction

Welcome to Chess & Checkers Online (the "App", "Application" or "Service").

Chess & Checkers Online is an online multiplayer game that allows users to play chess and checkers, compete with other players, communicate through in-game chat and, where enabled, communicate using real-time voice chat.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Application, why it is processed, which third-party services may be involved, and what choices you have regarding your information.

By using the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We May Process

Depending on which features you use, the Application may process different categories of information.

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

The Application may provide account authentication through Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.

This may include:

  • Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Email address, when provided by the authentication provider
  • Display name
  • Profile information provided by Google when Google Sign-In is used
  • Authentication and account status information

We use this information to authenticate users, maintain accounts, provide multiplayer functionality and associate gameplay activity with the correct account.

2.2 Gameplay and Multiplayer Information

When you participate in an online game, information necessary to operate the multiplayer functionality may be processed.

This may include:

  • Game or room identifier
  • Player identifiers
  • Player display names
  • Game type and game settings
  • Game moves and gameplay state
  • Game results and related statistics
  • Player status and connection-related information
  • Matchmaking information

This information is used to create, operate, synchronize and complete online games.

2.3 In-Game Chat

The Application may provide text-based communication between players. Messages sent through the game may be processed and temporarily or otherwise stored through the application's online infrastructure in order to deliver the messaging functionality.

Please do not share passwords, payment information, private contact information or other sensitive personal information through the in-game chat.

2.4 Voice Chat and Microphone

Chess & Checkers Online may provide real-time voice communication between players during an online game.

If you choose to use voice chat, the Application requires access to your device microphone.

Microphone access is requested when the voice functionality is used. You can refuse microphone access or disable microphone access through your device settings.

Voice communication is implemented using real-time WebRTC technology. The application infrastructure may use Firebase Firestore for connection signaling information required to establish the voice connection.

Firestore signaling information may include technical connection data such as session offers, answers and ICE connection candidates. The actual real-time audio stream is transmitted through the WebRTC connection rather than being stored by the Application as a recorded audio file.

The Application does not intentionally record or permanently store your voice conversations.

3 Firebase Services

The Application uses Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication, cloud storage and online multiplayer functionality.

Depending on the feature being used, Firebase may process information such as account identifiers, authentication information, gameplay information, chat-related data and technical connection information.

Firebase services are operated by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

More information is available in Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy .

Depending on your location, applicable law and your advertising preferences, personalized or non-personalized advertisements may be displayed.

5 In-App Purchases
